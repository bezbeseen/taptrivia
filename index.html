Rules
Scoring and win
Correct: +1. Wrong: -1. All 12 categories rotate in. No category more than 2 times in a row. Repeats from the same category stay at least 50 questions apart in that category. In reader rotation, the reader cannot score. No one knows is only on open questions. True/False and multiple choice are tapped on the card.
